About RepairNow

Our Mission

"Find the Right Technician. Fix It Fast."

RepairNow was created to eliminate the frustration of finding trustworthy, capable technicians for home appliances, electrical devices, computing equipment, and vehicle repairs. By connecting customers with verified local technicians through a transparent 5-factor matching engine, we guarantee reliable service without guesswork.

Book a Repair

Our Core Pillars

  • Rigorous Verification: Technicians must submit proof of experience, trade certifications, and location documents before getting approved.
  • Rule-Based Fair Matching: We match technicians based on skill compatibility (35%), availability (20%), geographic proximity (20%), proven rating (15%), and budget match (10%).
  • Live Status Progression: Complete tracking from the moment a technician is selected, on the way, arrived, repairing, and completed.
  • Genuine Feedback: Customers can only leave ratings on completed jobs, keeping service accountability at its highest.
